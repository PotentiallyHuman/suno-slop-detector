Caught a glimpse of something rare,
Flickered once and left me there,
Tried to trace it through the haze,
But it danced beyond my gaze.
Every step, a sideways glance,
Like a dream that won’t advance.

You move like rhythm in disguise,
Pulling me through colored highs,
I reach but you dissolve again,
Like laughter lost in wind.

So tell me what you mean,
When you spin and disappear,
I’m chasing what I’ve seen,
But it’s never really near.
You shimmer in between,
Then vanish with the year.
