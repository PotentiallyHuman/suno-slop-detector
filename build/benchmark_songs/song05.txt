A narrow road in fading light
A traveler walked toward the night
His coat was thin, the wind was blowing cold
He traced old maps with edges rolled

He reached a farm left bare by rain
Its fence still scented crops and grain
He stepped inside the savaged wooden frame
And felt old souls without a name

He sat where floorboards bowed with age
And sensed the time that was written on each page
A restless wind revealed his ache
Exposing dreams he dared not make

The tale he lived grew in his chest
A quiet truth he never guessed
He learned how miles and time reshape the way
A weary heart, of his, moves through the day

He breathed a vow beneath the beams
To follow roads that shaped his dreams
He let the silent rafters sway
And found a softer kind of way
