Paste your lyrics (or open them on suno.com) for an instant 0–100% read of how machine-generated they sound, with a breakdown of the clichés, stock phrases, predictable rhymes, and repetition that give AI away.

Then humanize them in one click:
• Humanize Line — rebuild your single worst line
• Humanize Rewrite — swap every cliché word at once
• Humanize Chaos — a full rework when you want it truly human

Your rhymes and hooks are kept, and it never makes a line read more AI than it started.

100% on your device — no account, no sign-in, nothing uploaded. It reads only your lyrics box. Free and open source.
